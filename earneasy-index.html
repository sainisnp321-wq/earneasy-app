Frequently asked questions

Online earning ko lekar kuch zaroori baatein.

Kya bina investment ke earning shuru ho sakti hai?

Kuch skills free resources se seekhi ja sakti hain, lekin internet/device ya practice ka samay lag sakta hai. Income guaranteed nahi hai.

Kya 2–3 din mein bahut paise kama sakte hain?

Aisi guarantee dene wale claims par bharosa na karein. Real income ke liye skill, trust, time aur consistent effort ki zaroorat hoti hai.

Kya EarnEasy job ya payment guarantee karta hai?

Nahi. EarnEasy educational guides aur general resources dikhata hai; kisi job, client, commission ya income ki guarantee nahi deta.

Kya affiliate link se commission milta hai?

Sirf tab jab aap kisi affiliate program mein accepted hon aur uske rules ke mutabik eligible action ho. Affiliate relationship clearly disclose karein.
